01 — About

Leading people, staying close to the code.

At CompuGroup Medical I lead two cross-functional product teams — 15 engineers across Germany and Romania — building CGM Praxis, a multi-tenant cloud SaaS for medical practices with deep TI/gematik integration. I own delivery, engineering practices and people development for both teams.

Before that I managed the Avira Password Manager team through its integration into Gen, and spent eight years at inhive as lead engineer, team lead and architect on healthcare, life sciences and aviation projects for Roche, Lufthansa Group, Genentech, Merck and Bayer.

I care about teams that ship steadily and engineers who keep growing. I stay hands-on — architecture reviews, CI/CD, automation — and use side projects to try new tools and AI workflows before bringing them to a team.
